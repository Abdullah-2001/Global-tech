Our process

From concept
to completion.

A structured six-stage process keeps creative vision, engineering and execution aligned.

01 — DISCOVER

Understand the place.

We study the location, surrounding environment, traffic flow, project objectives and brand identity.

02 — CONCEPT & PLANNING

Shape the idea.

Creative concepts, architectural directions and visualization options are developed around the location.

03 — DESIGN & VISUALIZATION

See it before it exists.

Detailed 3D visualizations help you understand how the landmark will look in its actual environment.

04 — ENGINEERING & DEVELOPMENT

Prepare for reality.

The selected concept is prepared for fabrication, structural requirements, materials, lighting and installation.

05 — CONSTRUCTION & INSTALLATION

Build the landmark.

Our team executes the approved concept and transforms the design into a physical landmark.

06 — ILLUMINATION & HANDOVER

Make it shine.

Lighting, finishing, landscaping and final details are completed for a landmark ready to make an impact.

Questions, answered

Before you
get in touch.

Straight answers to what new clients ask most.

Can you work with clients outside your own country?

Yes. Strategy, design and visualization are delivered online. For physical projects, we plan delivery around your site and local requirements.

What does a project cost?

Every project is scoped to its size, location and materials. Send a short brief and we will reply with a clear proposal.

Do you handle both branding and physical landmarks?

Yes. One team can take you from brand identity to a finished, illuminated landmark, so the story stays consistent.

What should I send to get started?

Your business goals, any existing logo or brand files, and, for landmarks, the location with a few photos or a map pin.
